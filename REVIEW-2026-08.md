# Technical Review — Zero-to-Junior Platform Engineer

**Reviewer:** Principal engineer review, 2026-08-24/25
**Method:** Claims checked against primary vendor documentation and RFCs, and — where possible — reproduced by execution on Ubuntu 24.04.4 / Docker 29.1.3 / bash 5.2.21 / systemd 255.4.
**Coverage:** 5 of 7 planned scopes complete. Weeks 6–7 (Python/Docker), Week 11–12 + RESOURCES link-check, and Multipass/infra were interrupted and are **not** covered — see §7.

---

## 1. Verdict

The course is unusually well written. The prose, the pedagogy, the troubleshooting method, and the honesty of the framing are genuinely strong, and a large majority of technical claims checked out against primary sources. The problem is not the teaching. It is that **the labs were never executed end to end.**

Every scope that was actually run produced learner-stopping defects. Confirmed so far:

| Scope | BLOCKER | MAJOR | MINOR | GAP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Weeks 04–05 · Networking, HTTP/TLS/LB | 9 | 11 | 10 | 12 |
| Weeks 08 + 10 · Compose, Swarm | 4 | 8 | 5 | 2 |
| Week 09 · Observability | 4 | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| Weeks 01–03 · Linux, systemd, Bash | 2 | 8 | 9 | 1* |
| **Total (partial)** | **19** | **35** | **32** | **23** |

\* aggregated; contains 10 sub-items.

**BLOCKER** = the learner cannot proceed; a command fails or a stated outcome cannot occur.

---

## 2. The systemic cause

`make check` passes. Verified on this host:

```
shellcheck: all clean       (21 scripts)
pytest:     39 passed
bats:       14/14 ok
mkdocs build --strict: exit 0, 0 broken links, 61/61 files in nav
```

The gate tests the **reference scripts**. It never stands up the Week 8 stack, never starts the Week 9 observability stack, never runs `nginx -t` on a lab config, and never runs a chaos drill to assert its symptom appears. Defects therefore cluster precisely where the gate does not look.

Two of the fourteen bats tests additionally assert nothing at all (§4.4), so even the covered surface is thinner than the green result suggests.

**This is the highest-leverage fix in the entire review.** A gate that executed the labs would have caught all 19 blockers.

## 3. Blockers, by week

### Weeks 04–05 — Networking, HTTP, TLS, load balancing

1. **`http2 on;` does not exist in the nginx the course installs.** `files/nginx-lab.conf:36`. Ubuntu 24.04 ships nginx 1.24.0; the standalone directive requires 1.25.1+. Reproduced:
   `nginx/1.24.0 → [emerg] unknown directive "http2"`, while nginx 1.31.4 accepts it and complains only about the missing certificate. The lab tells the learner to expect a *missing-certificate* error, so they conclude the lab is lying. **Fix:** `listen 443 ssl http2 default_server;` — valid in both.
2. **All of Part 2 talks to a server block that only returns 301.** `lab.md:88–127` vs `nginx-lab.conf:25-32`. With the 443 block commented out as instructed, the port-80 block is a bare redirect, so every `curl … | jq` in 2.2–2.5 fails.
3. **The 502 demonstration cannot produce a 502.** `lab.md:125` kills app1+app2, but `nginx-lab.conf:14` marks app3 `backup` — so nginx fails over and returns 200. Reproduced.
4. **The "wrong hostname" TLS test succeeds.** `make-ca.sh:59` unconditionally puts `127.0.0.1` in the SAN, and `lab.md:200` tests against `https://127.0.0.1/`.
5. **The "missing intermediate" test cannot fail** — `make-ca.sh` issues a two-level chain with no intermediate at all.
6. **The clock-skew test is backwards.** `lab.md:208` sets the clock *forward*, which yields "certificate has expired"; "not yet valid" (the stated expectation) requires going *backwards*.
7. **`dig` vs `getent` demo inverts on Ubuntu 24.04.** `lab.md:200-206`, labelled "**This is the lesson**". `dig` queries the systemd-resolved stub at 127.0.0.53, and resolved reads `/etc/hosts` (`ReadEtcHosts=yes`), so `dig beta` answers. Needs `dig @1.1.1.1 beta` to make the point.
8. **HAProxy is restarted on port 80 while nginx holds it.** `lab.md:228-234` — the "change bind to 8080" instruction comes *after* the restart, and `haproxy -c` does not bind, so validation gives a false green.
9. **Drill 04's symptom is impossible.** `04-network.sh:68` claims "I can ping beta"; fault 2 points `beta` at an unrouteable 10.99.99.99, so ping fails. The script's own answer file contradicts the symptom.

### Weeks 08 + 10 — Compose and Swarm

10. **The Week 8 stack cannot start.** `Makefile:12` writes the secret `chmod 600` owned by the host user; `api/Dockerfile:5-6` runs as uid 10001; Compose `file:` secrets preserve host uid/mode and have no `uid:`/`gid:`/`mode:` options outside Swarm. `api/app.py:30` does a bare `open().read()`, so the API dies at import. Parts 2–7 are unreachable.
11. **Compose merge semantics are taught backwards.** `README.md:225` — "lists are replaced (not appended)". The spec says sequences are **appended**. Independently reproduced: `cap_add` merged to `[NET_ADMIN, SYS_PTRACE]`; `command` overridden (the documented exception).
12. **`volumes: []` / `ports: []` in the prod overlay are silent no-ops.** Consequence: "prod" ships the dev bind-mount and a published Postgres port. Requires `!reset []`. Reproduced.
13. **The Week 8 drill's symptom is unproducible.** `08-compose.sh` rewrites env vars that exist nowhere in the stack, and `app.py` never connects to Postgres and has no 500 code path at all.

### Week 09 — Observability

14. **`host.docker.internal` does not resolve on Linux Docker Engine.** It is a Docker Desktop feature; the compose file never sets `extra_hosts: host-gateway`. Two of four scrape targets are permanently DOWN, silently killing every node_exporter-based alert — while `lab.md:76` says "Everything must be `UP`."
15. **Promtail reached EOL on 2026-03-02.** Confirmed at Grafana's docs: *"Promtail is end of life (EOL) as of March 2, 2026… you must migrate to Alloy or another supported client."* `:latest` hides a four-month version skew (promtail 3.6.8 vs loki 3.7.6).
16. **The official Promtail image cannot read the journal** (no `promtail_journal_enabled` build tag), so the `systemd-journal` job and two lab queries return nothing.
17. **`promtool check config` as written exits 1.** `prometheus.yml:9` uses absolute `/etc/prometheus/alerts.yml`; the lab mounts at `/p`.

### Weeks 01–03 — Linux, systemd, Bash

18. **Drill 03 ships its own answer key.** The heredoc payload written to `/opt/lab/backup.sh` contains all three planted faults, pre-numbered and explained. Confirmed by extraction. `cat /opt/lab/backup.sh` — the first thing anyone does — destroys the exercise.
19. **`backup.sh` silences its own stderr.** `:123` — `exec 9>"${LOCKFILE}" 2>/dev/null` permanently rebinds the shell's fd 2 (verified: brace-scoping preserves it, the bare form does not). Twelve log calls after it are discarded, including every error path and the trap's exit message. The script exits 1 with **zero output**. The week's stated goal is Bash that "fails loudly instead of silently"; `lab.md:288` promises the logging lands in the journal. **Fix:** `if ! { exec 9>"${LOCKFILE}"; } 2>/dev/null; then`.

## 4. Recurring patterns

### 4.1 Drills whose stated symptom their own faults cannot produce
Four of eleven confirmed: `04-network` (ping cannot work), `08-compose` (no 500 path exists), `09-latency` (qdisc on egress, reproduce command uses loopback), `10-swarm` (unsatisfiable constraint gives 0/6, not 4/6 — and "fault 3" is a comment followed by a line that only *removes* a container never created).

### 4.2 Answer leaks
`03-script.sh` (complete, pre-numbered) and `02-disk.sh` (payload left world-readable at `/opt/lab/.holder.sh`). Both violate `infra/chaos/README.md:11`.

### 4.3 Version drift against what Ubuntu 24.04 ships
nginx 1.24 vs `http2 on;`. Promtail EOL. cAdvisor moved from `gcr.io/cadvisor/cadvisor` (v0.55.1, stale) to `ghcr.io/google/cadvisor` (v0.60.5). node_exporter 1.7.0 from the archive vs 1.12.1 upstream. `825` days for a leaf cert (the limit has been 398 since 2020).

### 4.4 Tests that assert nothing
`backup.bats` test 3 runs `sha256sum -c` via `run` inside a discarded subshell and never checks `$status` — passes with a corrupted checksum. Test 14 targets `/tmp/backup.lock` while the script uses `/var/lock/backup.lock` (world-writable on Ubuntu, so the fallback never fires), and guards its only assertion behind `if [ "$status" -eq 1 ]` — passes with `flock` removed entirely.

### 4.5 The course contradicting itself
- `week-11:179` "**Never deploy `:latest`**" vs `:latest` across the Week 9 stack and Week 11's own Gitea lab.
- 503 mapped to "no healthy upstream" in `week-05/README.md:50`, but `week-05/solutions.md:14` correctly files that under 502 (nginx returns 502; HAProxy returns 503).
- `PENDING` defined as "the scheduler could not place it" two lines after a state chain showing it as a normal transient state.
- "`deploy:` is ignored by plain `docker compose up`" (Week 10) while Week 8 depends on `deploy.resources.limits` being honoured — Compose v2 does honour it.

### 4.6 Comments describing code that isn't there
A `:?` explainer above a line containing no `:?`; a "pinned so it competes" CPU hog with no `taskset`; "HAProxy chroots" above a `global` block with no `chroot`; "latency on the loopback path" for a qdisc on the egress interface; "Needed for Grafana Explore and recording rules" above `--web.enable-lifecycle`, which does neither.

## 5. Curriculum gaps

**Git is never taught.** Eight incidental mentions across twelve weeks; `git branch` and `git commit` appear in no week file. Yet Week 11 builds a CI pipeline triggered on push, `week-11/lab.md:154` runs `git rev-parse --short HEAD` to tag an image, and `week-12/README.md:54` grades "everything reproducible from a git repository". The stated prerequisite (clone/add/commit) does not carry a learner to a reviewed, tagged, revertible deploy.

**README §2 overstates drill coverage.** "Every week… ends with a Break/Fix drill" — Weeks 06 and 11 have none.

**Alertmanager is listed in the stack table and never appears.** `curl /api/v1/alertmanagers` against the running stack returns an empty list. The week teaches alert authoring and alert philosophy, then omits grouping, silences, inhibition and routing — which are the actual answer to the alert-fatigue argument it makes so well.

**`tmux`/`screen` never mentioned**, despite eight "terminal 1 / terminal 2" instructions and a Week 11 step that tells the learner to keep a safety-line SSH session open.

**Also absent:** SLOs/error budgets (present only in an optional challenge's solution), tracing/OpenTelemetry, recording rules, MTU/PMTUD, conntrack, `sudoers`/`visudo`, LVM and filesystem growth, `strace` (referenced twice as the answer, never introduced), AppArmor, `unattended-upgrades`, apt pinning proper.

**Repo-level:** no `LICENSE`, and no CI running `make check`.

## 6. Proposed sequence

**P0 — unblock the labs.** The 19 blockers in §3. Nothing else matters until a learner can complete Weeks 4, 5, 8, 9 as written.

**P1 — stop teaching false things.** Compose merge semantics; TIME_WAIT ownership and the `tcp_fin_timeout`/`tcp_tw_recycle` myths; `[::]` dual-stack binding; CN/SAN vs what curl actually accepts; 503-vs-502; `logrotate size` vs `maxsize`; `Persistent=` on monotonic timers; the `set -e` exceptions for `local x=$(...)` and functions called from conditions; `sudo -u X -s` vs `-i`.

**P2 — make the drills work.** Fix the four impossible symptoms, close the two answer leaks, and audit the remaining five the same way.

**P3 — currency pass.** Promtail → Grafana Alloy (`alloy convert` makes this mechanical, and makes a good exercise); pin every image; move cAdvisor to ghcr.io; decide on Ubuntu 26.04 vs 24.04; correct the 825-day cert comment.

**P4 — close the gaps.** A Git/PR module is the biggest single hole. Then Alertmanager, tmux, SLOs in the main text.

**P5 — prevent recurrence.** Extend `make check` to stand up the Week 8 and Week 9 stacks and assert health; run `nginx -t`/`haproxy -c` on every shipped config; run each drill and assert its symptom appears. Add a CI workflow that runs it. Add a `LICENSE`.

## 7. Not covered

Three scopes were interrupted by repeated session-limit and connection failures and are **not** represented above:

- **Weeks 11–12** — production/security/CI, capstone, plus the full external link check of `RESOURCES.md`. Given the hit rate elsewhere, assume defects here too.
